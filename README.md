## 📖 Overview
<a id="overview"></a>
Encore is a complete music notation application that allows you to create scores and compose comfortably. This is the full version of Encore with all features unlocked and updates included, ensuring you have the best tools at your fingertips. The software supports both MIDI and audio sequencing, making it ideal for classical musicians who prefer working with traditional notation, while also offering modern capabilities.

With compatibility for VST instruments and the ability to export your projects as audio (WAV) or MIDI, Encore provides a versatile environment for musicians. Additionally, it includes more than 30 templates to help you kickstart your projects, which can be customized by adding or removing instruments and parts.
